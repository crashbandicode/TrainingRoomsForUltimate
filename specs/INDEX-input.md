## Cross-area interfaces named in these specs

- Mash spec: Playback Slot actions, `is_playback_queued` / queued slot / reset, mash interrupt via `stop_playback` (SPEC-input-01 §5.4, §5.6).
- Defensive (ledge) spec: `playback_ledge(slot)` and the buffer schedule; cliff-climb transition disable while a playback ledge option is pending (SPEC-input-01 §5.3, §5.6).
- Save-state spec: stop on load request; record/playback-on-load after both fighters restore; chords consumed during load; the damage helper's "load in progress" signal (SPEC-input-01 §6.4, SPEC-input-05 open question 4).
- Menu spec: menu-open consumer, close grace, mod-menu and vanilla-menu flags (SPEC-input-04 §5, SPEC-input-02 §5.2).
- HUD spec: the HUD toggle and the Revision 29 anchors (SPEC-input-02 §4, SPEC-input-05 §4).
