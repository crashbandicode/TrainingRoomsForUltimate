*Practice games › Mash out › Start: the CPU grabs Mario again and again. Nobody mashes here, so each result at the top left reads “Poor · 90f”. Eden, install 6's diagnostic build `5df3d9dc`, 2026-11-26.* [Open the clip](../media/tut-mash-out.mp4)

Works in Training and in the [waiting room](waiting-rooms.md). Choose **Fighter and move** in [Practice games](../menu/practice-games.md#mash-out), then **Start**. The CPU performs the chosen capture; mash to escape and see your result.

Pick a fighter to see its available moves, or **Random** for a random state or a random move in one state. For a grab, choose Standing, Dash, Pivot or Random. Sora's Blizzard is practice only and does not give a grade.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| A | Open a fighter or Random, then choose a move. |
| Left/Right | Change the grab type in the move list. |
| B | Go back or close the move list. |
| Stick and buttons | Mash to escape the capture. |
