# Mash out

Works in Training and in the [waiting room](waiting-rooms.md). Choose **Fighter and move** in [Practice games](../menu/practice-games.md#mash-out), then **Start**. The CPU performs the chosen capture; mash to escape and see your result.

Pick a fighter to see its available moves, or **Random** for a random state or a random move in one state. For a grab, choose Standing, Dash, Pivot or Random. Some moves are practice only and do not give a grade.

<!-- CLIP mash-out-escape: The CPU captures Player 1, who mashes out and sees the result. -->

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| A | Open a fighter or Random, then choose a move. |
| Left/Right | Change the grab type in the move list. |
| B | Go back or close the move list. |
| Stick and buttons | Mash to escape the capture. |
