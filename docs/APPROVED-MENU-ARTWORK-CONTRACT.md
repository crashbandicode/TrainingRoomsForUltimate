# Approved menu artwork contract

2026-11-03. This records the existing operator-approved redesign; it does not approve a new design or claim runtime acceptance.

SPEC-core-07 section 9 originally asks for a picture pane and texture for every submenu identifier. That row refers to the older button/stacked-Icon layout described in SPEC-core-03 sections 2.2 and 4.2. The later MENU-DRAFT draft 9 and docs/MENU-PROPOSAL v11 explicitly record operator approval on November 1 and the remaining answers on November 2. Their current menu is a group column and text setting rows with the specified controls and preview chips.

The lead read the approval and control vocabulary and directly viewed the approved docs/menu-mockup/02-saves.png. Save States, Slot, Mirroring, Auto-load, damage and Buffs rows have text labels and controls, without the older submenu picture icons. The proposal Visual sources table explicitly replaces the DI/SDI/Airdodge/Shield Tilt icons with direction chips and the Character item icon with its placeholder. Kirby Hats, Solo Popo and Character items retain the approved neutral placeholder until their final icons are designed.

Accordingly the universal old submenu-icon/texture clause is superseded by this approved presentation contract. Do not introduce a new atlas of 42 icons or alter the approved layout solely to satisfy that older clause. This does not waive any required direction visual, item icon, runtime fighter portrait, runtime stage thumbnail, placeholder, control glyph, pane mapping or original-artwork provenance requirement. Those remain individually mapped and require their host/native evidence. Text-only coverage does not prove any of those image requirements.

The acceptance inventory keeps core07.2 as a superseded historical presentation clause; its final RC row is still open until the applicable approved rendering contract is validated. Asset digest reproduction core07.5 remains open and separate. No upstream source or artwork was opened, built or copied for this reconciliation.
